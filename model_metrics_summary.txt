INTERPRETATION

Logistic regression was selected as the final model because it provided
the strongest overall balance of recall, PR-AUC, and ROC-AUC for
identifying stroke cases in this highly imbalanced dataset.

On the unseen test set, the model correctly identified 40 of the 50
stroke cases, resulting in a recall of 0.800. However, precision was
relatively low at 0.138 because the model also classified 250 non-stroke
cases as stroke cases. This reflects the tradeoff between identifying a
high proportion of stroke cases and generating false-positive
predictions.

The high accuracy of the Dummy Classifier and Random Forest demonstrates
why accuracy alone was not appropriate for model selection. The Dummy
Classifier achieved 0.951 accuracy while identifying no stroke cases,
and the initial Random Forest also had extremely low recall. Therefore,
recall, PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, precision, F1-score, and the confusion matrix
were considered when evaluating model performance.
